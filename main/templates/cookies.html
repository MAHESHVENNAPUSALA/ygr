What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help us recognize your device, remember your preferences, and improve your overall browsing experience.

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several reasons, including:
To ensure our website functions correctly and efficiently.
To remember your settings and preferences for future visits.
To analyze website traffic and user behavior, helping us improve our services.
To personalize content and ads, making your experience more relevant.
To maintain security and detect any unauthorized access.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Necessary for website functionality.
Performance Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our site.
Functionality Cookies: Remember user preferences and settings.
Targeting/Advertising Cookies: Deliver relevant ads and measure their effectiveness.

Managing Cookies

You can choose to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser settings to decline them if you prefer. Please note, disabling cookies may limit some features and functionality of our site.
